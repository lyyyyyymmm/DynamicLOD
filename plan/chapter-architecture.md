# 中文稿章节架构

## Required chapter files

- chapters/01_introduction_related.md | min_chars=1800 | agent=required | placeholders=no
- chapters/02_method.md | min_chars=1800 | agent=required | placeholders=no
- chapters/03_experimental_design.md | min_chars=1500 | agent=required | placeholders=yes
- chapters/04_results.md | min_chars=600 | agent=required | placeholders=yes
- chapters/05_discussion_conclusion.md | min_chars=1200 | agent=required | placeholders=yes

`chapters/` 是 `paper/chapters_cn/` 的兼容目录入口，组装文件为 `paper/manuscript_zh.md`。章节内容必须与 `paper/manuscript_en.md` 的论点、公式、参数、引用键和证据状态一致，但中文语序应自然，不做逐句机械直译。
